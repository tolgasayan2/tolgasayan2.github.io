Head to Head: Liar Party · Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Support

We collect no personal data

The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. We do not collect, store or share any data about you or your device.

Motion sensor

To tell when the phone on your forehead is tipped, the game reads the device's motion sensor on the device, only while a round is running. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Camera and microphone (round video)

With "Film each round" on, the front camera and microphone record the team giving clues while the phone is on someone's forehead. The video is made and kept only on your phone and is never sent anywhere unless you share it. You can turn it off on the setup screen or revoke access in Settings. It is saved to your photo library only when you choose Save.

What stays on your device

Your team and player names, chosen decks, and language and sound settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchase

The download is free; extra decks unlock with a one-time App Store purchase. No subscription, no ads. Apple processes the payment; we never see payment details.

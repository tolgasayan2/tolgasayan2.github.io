Hush House Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.
  • We use PostHog (EU cloud, Frankfurt) for anonymous gameplay analytics: which floors are started, won, failed or left, so we can tune the difficulty. It is never linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and we never sell data.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. The Meta (Facebook) SDK measures whether our own ad campaigns lead to installs; it is not started at all in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared only if you allow Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ads load, and analytics starts only if you agree.

1. Who we are

Hush House (Turkish: Huzur Apartmanı) is a logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Tolga Sayan, an individual developer ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Tolga Sayan is the controller for the app. Contact: tolgasayan@icloud.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (current floor, hints, streak, stars and personal records, daily puzzle results), your settings (sound, haptics) and whether you bought No Ads are saved on your device, in the app's local storage. We don't receive this data, apart from the few summary values listed in section 6. It is deleted when you delete the app, and it is included in your normal device backup (iCloud or computer) under Apple's control.

3. What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or account of any kind.
  • No servers of our own. Anonymous analytics goes to PostHog (section 6), and nothing else is sent to us.
  • We don't sell data, and we never link analytics to who you are.
  • We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

The game is free and supported by banner, interstitial and optional reward-video ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To serve ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location is derived;
  • advertising data and interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ads;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see section 7).

Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices in the consent form (where it applies) and on the tracking permission. Because the game is rated 4+, we limit ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" rating). Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

5. Ad measurement: Meta (Facebook) SDK

We use the Meta SDK, from Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc., only to find out whether our own advertising campaigns on Meta's services lead to people installing and playing the game. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where GDPR applies, the Meta SDK is not started at all, so nothing is sent to Meta from the app there. Elsewhere, automatic event logging is turned off and the app sends Meta:

  • that the app was opened (which also lets Meta attribute the install to an ad);
  • a few game milestones, each at most once per install: finishing the tutorial, reaching floor 10 and reaching floor 30;
  • buying No Ads, with the price amount and currency you paid. It contains no name, Apple Account or payment card details.

Along with these events, the Meta SDK sends technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, time zone and IP address. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is sent to Meta only if you allow tracking. In the United States the app turns on Meta's Limited Data Use mode, and Meta applies its restrictions based on the state you are in. The Meta SDK starts only once ads are allowed to start; milestones reached before that are kept on your device and sent once it starts. Meta processes this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

On first launch the app also registers with Apple's SKAdNetwork, Apple's privacy-preserving install attribution. Apple reports aggregated, delayed results to ad networks without any identifier that points to you.

6. Analytics: PostHog

To learn where players get stuck and to tune the difficulty of the floors, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to PostHog, from PostHog Inc. We use PostHog's EU cloud, so the data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany. The app creates a random ID on your device for this; it is not your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier (IDFA), and we never connect it to who you are. Deleting the app deletes the ID.

What is sent:

  • gameplay events: viewing the rule cards, starting, winning, failing or leaving a floor (with the floor number, board size, time taken, hearts lost, hints and undos used, stars and whether it was a personal record), using a hint, opening the daily puzzle share sheet, opening the streak chest, showing or watching an ad (type only), and viewing, buying or restoring No Ads (no price or payment details);
  • a few summary values: highest floor reached, days since install, whether you own No Ads, app language and total stars;
  • app opened, backgrounded and installed or updated, with the app version;
  • from version 1.1, whether the install came from one of our Apple Search Ads campaigns, and if so the campaign, ad group, keyword ID and country. The app learns this from Apple's own attribution service (AdServices): a token created on your device is sent only to Apple, without the advertising identifier and without the tracking permission. Where GDPR applies, this check runs only with the same consent as analytics;
  • device model, operating system version, language, and the country derived from your IP address. IP addresses themselves are discarded when the data arrives ("anonymize IPs" is on) and are not stored.

There is no session recording, no screen recording, no autocapture of what you tap, and no surveys. Where GDPR applies, analytics starts only if you agree in Google's consent form to storing and accessing information on your device (TCF purpose 1), and it stops if you withdraw that consent (section 8). Elsewhere it starts when the game launches. PostHog processes this data on our behalf under its privacy policy.

7. Apple's tracking permission (ATT) and the IDFA

The IDFA is a device identifier that Apple controls. The game asks for Apple's "Allow tracking" permission once, after you have played the first floors, not when you first open it. If you tap Allow, Google AdMob and Meta may use the IDFA for advertising and measurement. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the app and the SDKs cannot access the IDFA, and the whole game stays available. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

8. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form at a natural pause after the first floors. Until you have answered it, no ads are requested and analytics doesn't start. Analytics starts only if you agree to storing and accessing information on your device. The Meta SDK never starts in these regions. Elsewhere, ads, analytics and the Meta SDK start when the game launches; the tracking permission in section 7 still applies everywhere.

Your choices in the form control how Google and its ad partners may use your data, for example for personalised or non-personalised ads, and whether our analytics runs. Where GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent. To review or withdraw your consent, open the game and go to Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy options (Gizlilik seçenekleri). This button appears wherever the form applies. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that took place before.

9. Purchases

No Ads is a single one-time purchase (a non-consumable in-app purchase) sold through the App Store. There is no subscription and nothing renews. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether you own No Ads. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your device (around 12:30 and 20:00, only for the week after you last opened it). Nothing is sent to a server, and no push token is collected. You can turn them off in your device's Settings › Notifications.

11. Children

The game is rated 4+, which means it contains no objectionable content. It is a general-audience game and is not part of the App Store Kids category. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children; the analytics described in section 6 is anonymous and never linked to a person. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us at tolgasayan@icloud.com. You can also decline the tracking permission and, where it applies, consent in the form, and you can restrict tracking through Apple's Screen Time settings.

12. Data retention

  • On your device: game data stays until you delete the app.
  • PostHog: anonymous analytics events are kept in our PostHog project only as long as they help us improve the game. We hold no other data about you.
  • Google and Meta keep the data they collect for the periods described in their own privacy policies linked above.

13. International transfers

Google and Meta may process the data described above on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies. PostHog stores our analytics data in its EU cloud in Frankfurt, Germany.

14. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in the EU/EEA, your local supervisory authority; in the UK, the ICO; in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board, KVKK Kurulu). Under KVKK Article 11 you may also ask whether your data is processed, for what purpose, and to whom it is transferred.

Because the analytics data we hold is anonymous, we usually cannot identify you or find your data. You can:

  • delete all game data by deleting the app;
  • withdraw ad and analytics consent in Settings › Privacy options in the game (where it applies), and turn off tracking in your device's settings;
  • exercise your rights over data held by Google or Meta through the tools in their privacy policies;
  • contact us at tolgasayan@icloud.com; we will reply within the time the law requires and help where we can.

15. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app.

16. Contact

Tolga Sayan · tolgasayan@icloud.com

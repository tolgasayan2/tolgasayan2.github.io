Hush House Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. Your progress stays on your device.
  • We don't run any servers or analytics of our own, we don't receive your personal data, and we never sell it.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. The Meta (Facebook) SDK measures whether our own ad campaigns lead to installs.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared only if you allow Apple's tracking prompt.
  • Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ads load and before Meta starts.

1. Who we are

Hush House (Turkish: Huzur Apartmanı) is a logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Tolga Sayan, an individual developer ("we", "us"). For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Tolga Sayan is the controller for the app. Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (current floor, hints, streak, daily puzzle results), your settings (sound, haptics) and whether you have an active subscription are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you delete the app, and it is included in your normal device backup (iCloud or computer) under Apple's control.

3. What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or account of any kind.
  • No servers of our own and no analytics of our own: the app doesn't send your data to us.
  • We don't sell your personal data, and we don't receive it in the first place.
  • We don't ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

The game is free and supported by banner, interstitial and optional reward-video ads served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To serve ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, language and app version);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location is derived;
  • advertising data and interactions, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ads;
  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (see section 6).

Whether ads are personalised depends on your choices in the consent form (where it applies) and on the tracking permission. Because the game is rated 4+, we limit ads to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" rating). Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses data for advertising

5. Ad measurement: Meta (Facebook) SDK

We use the Meta SDK, from Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc., only to find out whether our own advertising campaigns on Meta's services lead to people installing and playing the game. Automatic event logging is turned off. The app sends Meta:

  • that the app was opened (which also lets Meta attribute the install to an ad);
  • a few game milestones, each at most once per install: finishing the tutorial, reaching floor 10, reaching floor 30, and starting a subscription. The subscription event contains no price or payment details.

Along with these events, the Meta SDK sends technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, time zone and IP address. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is sent to Meta only if you allow tracking. The Meta SDK is not started until ads are allowed to start (see section 7); milestones reached before that are kept on your device and sent once it starts. Meta processes this data under the Meta Privacy Policy.

On first launch the app also registers with Apple's SKAdNetwork, Apple's privacy-preserving install attribution. Apple reports aggregated, delayed results to ad networks without any identifier that points to you.

6. Apple's tracking permission (ATT) and the IDFA

The IDFA is a device identifier that Apple controls. The game asks for Apple's "Allow tracking" permission once, after you have played the first floors, not when you first open it. If you tap Allow, Google AdMob and Meta may use the IDFA for advertising and measurement. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the app and the SDKs cannot access the IDFA, and the whole game stays available. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

7. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR)

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to show a consent form at a natural pause after the first floors. Until you have answered it, no ads are requested and the Meta SDK does not start. Elsewhere, ads and the Meta SDK start when the game launches; the tracking permission in section 6 still applies everywhere.

Your choices in the form control how Google and its ad partners may use your data, for example for personalised or non-personalised ads. Where GDPR applies, personalised advertising relies on your consent. To review or withdraw your consent, open the game and go to Settings (Ayarlar) › Privacy options (Gizlilik seçenekleri). This button appears wherever the form applies. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that took place before.

8. Purchases

Hush House Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your device (around 12:30 and 20:00, only for the week after you last opened it). Nothing is sent to a server, and no push token is collected. You can turn them off in your device's Settings › Notifications.

10. Children

The game is rated 4+, which means it contains no objectionable content. It is a general-audience game and is not part of the App Store Kids category. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children ourselves; the app collects no personal data on our behalf at all. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. You can also decline the tracking permission and, where it applies, consent in the form, and you can restrict tracking through Apple's Screen Time settings.

11. Data retention

  • On your device: game data stays until you delete the app.
  • With us: we hold no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.
  • Google and Meta keep the data they collect for the periods described in their own privacy policies linked above.

12. International transfers

Google and Meta may process the data described above on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

13. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in the EU/EEA, your local supervisory authority; in the UK, the ICO; in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Board, KVKK Kurulu). Under KVKK Article 11 you may also ask whether your data is processed, for what purpose, and to whom it is transferred.

Because we don't hold any personal data, we usually cannot identify you. You can:

  • delete all game data by deleting the app;
  • withdraw ad consent in Settings › Privacy options in the game (where it applies), and turn off tracking in your device's settings;
  • exercise your rights over data held by Google or Meta through the tools in their privacy policies;
  • contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}; we will reply within the time the law requires and help where we can.

14. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect in the app.

4. Reklamlar: Google AdMob

Oyun ücretsizdir; Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited bünyesindeki Google AdMob'un gösterdiği banner, geçiş ve isteğe bağlı ödüllü video reklamlarla desteklenir. Reklam sunmak, ölçmek, ne sıklıkta gösterildiğini sınırlamak ve kötüye kullanımı önlemek için Google Mobile Ads SDK'sı şunları toplayabilir:

  • cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri (ör. cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, uygulama sürümü);
  • IP adresin ve ondan çıkarılan yaklaşık konum;
  • reklam verileri ve etkileşimleri (hangi reklamın gösterildiği, dokunup dokunmadığın gibi);
  • reklamlarla ilgili tanılama ve performans verileri;
  • cihaz kimlikleri; reklam kimliği (IDFA) yalnızca izleme iznini verirsen (bkz. bölüm 6).

Reklamların kişiselleştirilip kişiselleştirilmeyeceği onay ekranındaki seçimlerine (geçerli olduğu yerlerde) ve izleme iznine bağlıdır. Oyun 4+ yaş derecelendirmesine sahip olduğu için reklamları genel izleyiciye uygun içerikle (Google'ın "G" derecesi) sınırlarız. Google bu verileri kendi politikalarına göre işler:

  • Google Gizlilik Politikası
  • Google'ın, hizmetlerini kullanan site ve uygulamalardan gelen bilgileri nasıl kullandığı
  • Google'ın reklamcılıkta verileri nasıl kullandığı

5. Reklam ölçümü: Meta (Facebook) SDK

Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. / Meta Platforms, Inc.'in Meta SDK'sını yalnızca Meta hizmetlerinde yürüttüğümüz kendi reklam kampanyalarımızın oyunun kurulup oynanmasına yol açıp açmadığını görmek için kullanırız. Otomatik olay kaydı kapalıdır. Uygulama Meta'ya şunları gönderir:

  • uygulamanın açıldığını (Meta kurulumu bir reklama bu sayede bağlayabilir);
  • kurulum başına en fazla birer kez olmak üzere birkaç oyun adımını: eğitimi bitirme, 10. kata ulaşma, 30. kata ulaşma ve abonelik başlatma. Abonelik olayı fiyat ya da ödeme bilgisi içermez.

Meta SDK'sı bu olaylarla birlikte cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi sürümü, dil, saat dilimi ve IP adresi gibi teknik bilgileri de gönderir. Reklam kimliği (IDFA) Meta'ya yalnızca izleme iznini verirsen gönderilir. Meta SDK'sı, reklamların başlamasına izin verilene kadar başlatılmaz (bkz. bölüm 7); bundan önce ulaşılan adımlar cihazında tutulur ve SDK başladığında gönderilir. Meta bu verileri Meta Gizlilik Politikası'na göre işler.

Uygulama ilk açılışta Apple'ın gizliliği koruyan kurulum ölçüm sistemi SKAdNetwork'e de kaydolur. Apple, reklam ağlarına seni tanımlayan bir kimlik olmadan, toplu ve gecikmeli sonuçlar bildirir.

6. Apple izleme izni (ATT) ve IDFA

IDFA, Apple'ın kontrol ettiği bir cihaz kimliğidir. Oyun Apple'ın "İzlemeye izin ver" iznini bir kez, ilk katları oynadıktan sonra sorar; oyunu ilk açtığında sormaz. İzin Ver'e dokunursan Google AdMob ve Meta IDFA'yı reklam ve ölçüm için kullanabilir. Uygulamadan İzlememesini İste'yi seçersen uygulama ve SDK'lar IDFA'ya erişemez; oyunun tamamı yine açıktır. Bu tercihi istediğin zaman cihazında Ayarlar › Gizlilik ve Güvenlik › İzleme'den değiştirebilirsin.

GDPR'ın geçerli olduğu yerlerde kişiselleştirilmiş reklamcılık senin onayına dayanır. Onayını gözden geçirmek veya geri çekmek için oyunda Ayarlar › Gizlilik seçenekleri'ne dokun. Bu düğme onay ekranının geçerli olduğu her yerde görünür. Onayı geri çekmek, öncesinde yapılan işlemeyi etkilemez.
